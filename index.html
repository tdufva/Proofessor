What it does

Starts gently Guides a new user through folder choice, deep scan, three sample suggestions, and a tiny demo CV export.
Finds material Looks through inbox, workspace, chosen folders, and subfolders; reads PDFs, text, CV material, BibTeX, DOI snippets, and course feedback when possible.
Reviews first Shows Aalto dimension, confidence, why suggested, proof found, missing pieces, and why the app found the item before anything becomes dossier evidence.
Designs CVs Lets the user choose a purpose: Aalto/TENK formal, elegant academic, research, artistic portfolio, teaching, compact two-page, or public profile.
Autofills profiles Extracts name, title, affiliation, themes, ORCID, Scholar, Aalto profile, selected publications, and exhibitions/projects.
Previews exports Shows page preview, typography, spacing density, section order, page estimate, and too-long or too-sparse warnings before export.
Exports dossiers Builds CV variants, evidence indexes, readiness checks, evidence-backed narrative paragraphs, and export packets from the approved record set.
Writes bios Creates editable academic, artistic, research-focused, teaching-focused, public-profile, and speaker-intro bios using the saved profile details.
