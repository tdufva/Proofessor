No bundled personal data. The app is local-first: files stay on the user's Mac unless they choose profile links or external sources.

What It Does

It scans your folders, suggests evidence, and helps you decide what belongs in a tenure dossier.

Proofessor starts with a guided flow: choose a folder, scan it, review sample suggestions, and export a tiny demo CV. After that, it helps collect publications, teaching material, artistic work, funding, service, leadership, recognition, and source traces into one reviewable evidence base.

Why It Is Useful

Tenure evidence is usually scattered. Proofessor makes it inspectable, editable, and easier to trust.

Instead of filling an empty database, the user reviews suggestions like an inbox. Each item shows why it matters, what proof was found, what is missing, and which Aalto-style dimension it may support. The user can accept, edit, dismiss, merge duplicates, or mark proof needed before exporting anything formal.

Features

Guided start

Choose a folder, run a deep scan, review three sample suggestions, and export a tiny demo CV so the app makes sense in the first ten minutes.

Best for: understanding the workflow without reading first.

Deep evidence scan

Looks through the workspace, inbox, chosen folders, and subfolders. It reads PDFs, text files, CV material, BibTeX, DOI snippets, folder names, and course feedback when possible.

Best for: turning existing files into editable suggestions.

Aalto-aware review

Shows the suggested Aalto dimension, confidence, reason, proof found, missing proof, file path, date, text snippet, and source link for each item.

Best for: deciding whether the evidence is actually useful.

Autofill My Profile

Extracts name, title, affiliation, research themes, artistic themes, teaching themes, ORCID, Scholar, Aalto profile, selected publications, and selected projects.

Best for: making CVs, bios, and dossiers more accurate from the start.

CV Design Studio

Choose the purpose instead of formatting manually: Aalto/TENK formal CV, elegant academic CV, research CV, artistic portfolio CV, teaching CV, compact two-page CV, or public profile.

Best for: producing the right CV for the situation.

Export preview

Preview typography, spacing density, section order, page count, and too-long or too-sparse warnings before exporting the final file.

Best for: catching problems before sharing formal material.

Bios and dossier narratives

Create academic, artistic, research, teaching, public-profile, and speaker-introduction bios, plus evidence-backed paragraphs for research/artistic work, teaching, impact, leadership, trajectory, and recognition.

Best for: writing from confirmed evidence instead of memory.

Privacy

Local review first, exports second.

Proofessor is designed as a local-first app. The shared package is clean: it does not include the developer's data, documents, scans, or private workspace.

Only share exports intentionally. The workspace itself should remain private.
