Privacy

Proofessor is local-first. The shared download should be empty of personal records and ready for a new user.

  • The app creates an empty workspace in the user's Documents folder.
  • Evidence records, attachments, profile links, and generated CV or dossier files stay in that local workspace.
  • The shared download should not include a user's workspace or personal evidence.
  • The app may open public profile links or archive web pages only when the user has configured those sources.
  • Calendar, mail, document-folder, profile, and public-web source settings are user-controlled.

Users are responsible for checking whether their institution allows a given source to be used in a tenure dossier.
